If they play again later, you'll see a one-line pointer back up to that profile instead of reading the same bio twice.

Moldover (Matthew Moldover, b. 1980) is an electronic musician and instrument designer based in San Francisco, credited as a pioneer and namer of "controllerism." WHY BMRI THINKS THEY'RE INTERESTING
